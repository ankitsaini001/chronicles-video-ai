FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need video editing skills?
No. You type an idea or paste notes, review the storyboard, and ByteReel makes the video.
Which languages are supported?
English, Hindi (हिंदी) and Hinglish. For every video you choose a female or male voice.
How long are the videos?
Between 60 and 90 seconds, in vertical 1080 × 1920 format.
Can I post directly to Instagram?
Yes. Connect your Instagram professional account to post now or schedule. You can also download the MP4.
Is ByteReel free?
Yes, ByteReel is free during the private beta.

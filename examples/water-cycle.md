# How the water cycle works

- **Type:** Process (evaluation topic 1, PIPELINE_DESIGN.md §11)
- **Language:** Indian English
- **Narrator:** Female first, then switch to Male
- **Audience:** Curious school students and adults in India; no science background needed

## Notes

Explain how the water cycle works as one continuous loop:

1. **Evaporation:** the Sun heats oceans, lakes and rivers; water turns into invisible vapour and rises.
2. **Condensation:** higher up the air is colder; vapour cools into tiny droplets that together form clouds.
3. **Precipitation:** droplets join, become too heavy and fall as rain, snow or hail.
4. **Collection:** water flows into rivers, soaks into the ground as groundwater and returns to the sea.

Mention roughly how Earth's water is split: about 97% oceans (salt water), about 2% ice, about 1% fresh water in the ground, lakes and rivers.

End with the idea that the same water keeps moving around the planet again and again.
